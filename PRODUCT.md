# NoPanic Projects

## Product

NoPanic Projects helps South African primary schools give families enough runway to prepare for projects. The first pilot serves one Bloemfontein-area primary school for one term.

## Audience

- Teachers who need to publish classroom projects quickly.
- Parents of Grade 1–7 learners who manage the practical work at home.
- School administrators who need a dependable, low-support system that understands SA-SAMS.

## Register

Product UI: clear, reassuring, editorial, and human. The emotional promise is calm preparation, not administration software. Use confident plum as the signature and warm yellow as a small moment of optimism.

## Principles

1. Make the next useful action obvious.
2. Replace deadline anxiety with visible runway.
3. Respect teachers’ time: publish in under two minutes.
4. Keep family data private and scoped to the right school and child.
5. Make operational health visible without making users think about infrastructure.

## Anti-references

Avoid corporate school-management dashboards, dense data tables, generic “AI SaaS” gradients, noisy gamification, and childish classroom clip-art.
