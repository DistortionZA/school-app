# NoPanic Projects Design System

## Direction

Tuesday afternoon in a sunlit primary-school staff room: practical, warm, composed, and reassuring. The product uses a restrained light interface so plum typography and small golden signals carry the identity.

## Colors

- Primary plum: `oklch(0.360 0.147 340)`
- Primary dark ink: `oklch(0.280 0.110 340)`
- Body ink: `oklch(0.240 0.025 340)`
- Accent gold: `oklch(0.720 0.140 55)`
- Pure white background: `oklch(1 0 0)`
- Surface: `oklch(0.985 0.005 340)`
- Muted text: `oklch(0.480 0.025 340)`

## Typography

DM Sans handles interface text. Fraunces is used sparingly for the main page heading and modal heading, adding a human editorial voice without harming scanability.

## Components

Use small-radius controls (6–9px), full-width list dividers, strong text hierarchy, and restrained motion. Avoid nested cards, decorative gradients, and oversized rounded containers. Focus states must remain visible and all controls must have labels or accessible names.
